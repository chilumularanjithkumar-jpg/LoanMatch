Eligibility & documents

Keep These Documents Ready

Basic eligibility: Indian resident, age 21–60 years, regular income, and a CIBIL score of 700+ preferred.

Salaried Employees

  • PAN card and Aadhaar card
  • Last 3 months' salary slips
  • Last 6 months' bank statement (salary account)
  • Form 16 or latest ITR
  • Passport-size photograph

Minimum net salary usually ₹15,000–₹25,000 per month, depending on the lender and city.

Self-employed / Business Owners

  • PAN card and Aadhaar card
  • Last 2 years' ITR with computation
  • Last 12 months' current account statement
  • GST registration or Udyam certificate
  • Shop / business address proof

Business should usually be running for at least 2–3 years.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Does enquiring with LoanMatch affect my CIBIL score?

No. Sending an enquiry does not affect your score. A lender checks your credit report only when you choose to apply with them.

Do you charge any fee for your service?

No. Our guidance is free for borrowers. Lenders may charge their own processing fee, which we show you clearly before you apply.

How quickly can I get the loan?

Gold loans can be disbursed the same day. Personal loans usually take 2–5 working days and home loans 1–3 weeks, depending on documents and property checks.

What CIBIL score do I need?

A score of 750 or above gets the best rates. Many lenders accept 700+, and some NBFCs consider lower scores with a higher rate.

Can self-employed people and shop owners apply?

Yes. Business owners, traders, professionals and shop owners can apply with ITR, bank statements and GST or Udyam registration.
